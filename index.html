About

I work at the University of Messina, where my research sits at the intersection of distributed systems, the Internet of Things and industrial automation. My background is in cloud-based IoT infrastructures: my university thesis, developed with an industrial partner for the monitoring of electrical substations, combined an OpenStack cloud with the Stack4Things framework, embedded boards and a cross-platform mobile app talking Bluetooth Low Energy.

Since then I have extended that foundation towards edge orchestration (Kubernetes/k3s, Crossplane, WebAssembly), low-power wireless networking (LoRaWAN, RPL, Thread) and trustworthy AI for cyber-physical systems. I care about work that can be rerun by someone else: every repository below ships with installation steps, configuration tables, tests where possible and an honest list of limitations.

Research activities

  • Execution-time validation for industrial CPS

    AIS/XAIR: a reference runtime for execution-time validation of context-carrying action intents in industrial cyber-physical systems. Runtime, experiments and frozen data are public.

    LD1-execution-gap-xair

  • Server-independent LoRaWAN roaming

    A bridge that lets devices roam across independent LoRaWAN network servers (ChirpStack with LWN-Simulator), consolidated as the reference implementation of a journal paper.

    lorawan-server-independent-roaming

  • Human evaluation of CPS reasoning

    CPS-HumanBench: the web version of a blinded human rating study, available in Italian and English.

    LD4-humanbench-rating-study

  • AI-generated text detection for academic writing

    An OR-ensemble of domain-tuned DeBERTa models that outperforms open detectors on Claude-generated text and on the RAID benchmark, with sentence highlighting, explanations and LaTeX tracked-changes tooling.

    ai-text-detector

  • Research planning assistant

    “Idea in, verified research plan out”: open sources only, no API keys, local LLMs and a citation guard that rejects unverifiable references.

    Research_AI

  • Stack4Things in the cloud-edge continuum

    Deployment and integration of Stack4Things with Kubernetes/k3s, Crossplane and WebAssembly, plus teaching material for the book Assembling Smart Cyber-Physical Systems.

    stack4things-crossplane-deployment k3s-wasm-edge-orchestration assembling-cps-training

Teaching & community

I design hands-on laboratories that students can run on their own laptop: each lab states its learning goals, gives exact commands, shows the expected output and ends with exercises. Topics include computer networks (transport, application layer, Wireshark, Mininet), industrial IoT protocols, low-power wireless (RPL, Thread) and programming fundamentals. I also maintain training material and code companions for the book Assembling Smart Cyber-Physical Systems (Stack4Things modules and chapters 13 and 16).
